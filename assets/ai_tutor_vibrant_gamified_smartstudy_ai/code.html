Why does 2x + 5 = 15 mean x = 5?

psychology

Great question! Let's solve it step-by-step together:

1

Subtract 5 from both sides

15 - 5 = 10
2

We are left with

2x = 10
3

Divide both sides by 2

10 ÷ 2 = 5

So, x = 5! Make sense?
